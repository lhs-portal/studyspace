4.2.2 Reasons for government spending

  • Main areas of spending: include public services (healthcare and education), public and merit goods, infrastructure (transport and energy), welfare transfers (pensions and unemployment benefits), defence and public safety, and debt interest payments.
  • Reasons and effects: To supply public and merit goods, boost total demand during economic downturns, reduce income inequality via welfare transfers, and enhance national productivity through long-term infrastructure investment.
