4.7.2 Measurement of inflation

  • Consumer Prices Index (CPI): The primary metric used to measure inflation by tracking price changes for a representative "basket of goods and services" consumed by average households.
  • CPI Measurement Process:
  • Selecting the Basket: A selection of commonly purchased goods and services based on household expenditure surveys.
  • Weighting: Assigning weights to items based on their proportion of total household income spent, ensuring price changes in major categories (e.g., housing, food) have a greater impact on the overall index.
  • Base Year & Index Calculation: Comparing the current cost of the weighted basket against a chosen base year (set at an index value of 100) to calculate the percentage inflation rate over time.
