3.1.1 Money

Forms of money

Money is an item which is generally acceptable as a means of payment.

The main forms of money used in most countries are coins. banknotes and bank accounts. Coins are often used to make small purchases and are given in change. Banknotes are used to buy more expensive items. In most countries, the main form of money is bank accounts.

There are a number of ways of transferring money from one bank account to another. These include direct debits. credit cards and transactions using a smartphone.

Legal tender is any form of payment which, by law. has to be accepted in settlement of a debt. Coins (up to a certain value) and banknotes are legal tender.

Digital currency is becoming increasingly important. There are three types of digital

currency:

  • Cryptocurrency is based online and does not have notes and coins.They are used mainly for investment (people buy, store and sell cryptocurrencies to make a profit) and can be used as a method of payment.
  • Stablecoins were developed 10 avoid fluctuations in value. They do this by linking their value to a currency, such as the US dollar, or a basket of goods. Stablecoins can be used to make payments.
  • Central bank digital currencies (CBDCs). CBDCs may be used as an additional method of payment alongside cash.

    • Functions of money

    Money has four functions.

  • Medium of exchange: Money allows people to buy and sell products. Products are exchanged for money, and that money is used to buy other products.
  • Store of value: Acting as a store of value means that money can be saved. Money, does not deteriorate with time and so will be acceptable in the future. It holds its value for a long time, allowing us to save it for future purposes.
  • Unit of account / measure of value: Money can also be used to place a value on an item. It enables buyers and sellers to agree on what items are worth relative to each other.
  • Standard of deferred payments: Money allows people 10 borrow and lend. Someone who wants 10 buy something now can get it by borrowing money from someone who does not want to use ii now.
