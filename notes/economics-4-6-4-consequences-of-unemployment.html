4.6.4 Consequences of unemployment

  • For individuals: Lower personal income, reduced standard of living, loss of work skills, and increased mental stress or social isolation.
  • For producers/firms: Reduced consumer spending leads to lower sales revenue and profits, though firms may benefit from a larger pool of job applicants.
  • For the government: Decreased tax revenue (from income tax and spending taxes) combined with increased expenditure on unemployment welfare payments.
  • For the economy: Underutilisation of economic productive capacity, resulting in lower national real output (GDP) and wasted human resources.
