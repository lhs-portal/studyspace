4.2.6 Effects of fiscal policy on government macroeconomic aims

  • Economic growth & Employment: Expansionary fiscal policy increases consumer disposable income and government spending, expanding real GDP and boosting demand for labour to reduce cyclical unemployment.
  • Price stability: Contractionary fiscal policy reduces total demand to control demand-pull inflation and maintain stable prices.
  • Redistribution of income: Progressive direct taxes combined with increased welfare spending redistribute wealth and lower poverty.
  • Environmental sustainability & balance of payments: Higher tariffs and indirect taxes on demerit goods encourage green choices and regulate import demand.
