4.6.3 Causes/types of unemployment

  • Frictional unemployment: Short-term unemployment experienced by individuals who are between jobs or entering the labour market.
  • Structural unemployment: Long-term unemployment caused by structural shifts in the economy, technological advances, or international competition causing a mismatch between worker skills and available job roles.
  • Cyclical unemployment: Unemployment resulting from a lack of aggregate demand during economic downturns or recessions.
  • Seasonal unemployment: Occurs when demand for workers fluctuates depending on specific times of the year, such as tourism, agriculture, or retail seasons.
