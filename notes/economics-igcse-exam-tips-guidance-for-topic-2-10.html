Economics ? 2. The Allocation of Resources

IGCSE Exam Tips & Guidance for Topic 2.10

Key assessment insights for answering IGCSE Paper 1 and Paper 2 questions on government intervention and indirect taxation:

  • Diagram Labels (AO2): Always clearly label axes (Price, Quantity), curves (D, S, S1), and equilibrium points (P, P1, Q, Q1) to ensure full marks on structured questions.
  • Analysing Indirect Taxation (AO2): Explain step-by-step how a tax increases costs of production, causing the supply curve to shift leftward (S to S1), raising market price (P to P1), and reducing quantity demanded (Q to Q1).
  • Two-Sided Evaluation (AO3 / Part d Questions): When answering "Discuss whether" questions (e.g. on imposing taxes on demerit goods), consider both the benefits (e.g., reduces consumption, generates government revenue) and limitations (e.g., inelastic demand may limit quantity fall, risk of illegal markets).
