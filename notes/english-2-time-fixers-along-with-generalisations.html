2. Time fixers (along with generalisations)

  • Beginning the introduction with a time fixer can be a simple yet effective strategy to get started as far as article writing is concerned.
  • Time fixers shed light on the importance of the topic at the point of time mentioned and they should be separated from the rest of the sentence with a comma.
  • A generalisation phrase would go hand in hand with a time fixer when beginning the introduction and this would surely make the sentence sound more mature.

  • Two time fixers can be used to show contrast and create interest if the writer wants to emphasise the change in situation about the topic in hand.

Example: In the past, families spent more quality time together. These days, they struggle to spare a few moments for their loved ones due to the hectic lifestyle.
