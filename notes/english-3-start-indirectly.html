3.Start indirectly

  • You may begin the introduction with an idea that is prominent but relevant to the actual topic in hand (not directly with the topic). For instance, starting an article on the topic of ‘building an airport’ with the idea of ‘airport’ will not sound very interesting. Thus, a good point to start an article on this particular topic could be ‘how transport has evolved’ or ‘significance of air transport in the present day’ Nonetheless, it is vital to bear in mind that the actual topic in question must be clear for the reader by the end of the introduction.

What should come after the introductory sentence? 

  • In spite of the fact that the second sentence depends on the type of beginning and the idea presented in it, it is often regarded as a good idea to follow the introductory sentence with the writer’s opinion (one sided articles) about the topic as portrayed in the example below.

Who wouldn’t wish to be able to splash out on chic clothes and trendy gadgets? Like many of you, I believe a moneyed man is far more likely to be happier than a pauper, and thus the role money plays in bringing felicity can never be doubted.

  • When it comes to balanced articles, the introduction is an ideal place to highlight the discursive nature of the topic. Therefore it is best to follow a general introductory sentence (complying with the qualities stated above) with a transition sentence like one from the list below.
  • If you are writing a two-sided article, DO NOT give your opinion in the introduction. 

Example transition sentences

1. ,but the idea of wearing uniforms to school has inflamed serious debates among some parents and students.

2. ,but not everyone is convinced about its benefits.

3. ,but there are some people who take a sceptical view towards this notion.

 4. ,but the overall effects of modern science, according to some people, are not as pleasing as it seems.

 5. ,but along with these glorious benefits, science also brings a great deal of worries.

 6. Sceptics, however, advocate that the benefits of continuing to study are unfounded.

7. The idea of sending a sports team abroad is one of the hottest topics in the present day.

8. However, this notion has surfaced mixed opinions from students and educators.

Model Introductions

Use of internet by students

It will not be an exaggeration to say that the advent of internet has proven to be one of the biggest boons of a student’s life. With internet at our disposal, our everyday struggles like bewildering homework problems can become a thing of the past. However, not everyone is convinced about its benefits.

Is car the ideal means of transport?

Transportation has always been the cornerstone of human activities. In this regard, car is unquestionably one of the most fast-developing and preferred means to travel. However, the idea of using car as a conventional way to travel cuts both ways.

Body

The structure if you are writing a one-sided article:

 Body paragraph 1: First idea supporting your opinion with an explanation

Body paragraph 2: Second idea supporting your opinion with an explanation (should be different from the first idea)
