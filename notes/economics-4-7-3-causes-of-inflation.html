4.7.3 Causes of inflation

  • Demand-pull inflation: Occurs when aggregate demand for goods and services outpaces aggregate supply, caused by factors such as excessive money supply growth, lower interest rates, increased consumer expenditure, or expansionary fiscal spending.
  • Cost-push inflation: Occurs when production costs rise for firms, forcing them to increase prices to maintain profit margins. Caused by rising raw material prices, higher wages, increased indirect taxation, or imported inflation due to currency depreciation.
