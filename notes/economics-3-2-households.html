Economics ? 3. Microeconomic decision makers

3.2 ? Households

Wealth is a stock of assets including money held in bank accounts, shares in companies, government bonds, cars and property.

Disposable income is the income of a person after all income-related taxes and charges have been deducted.

Consumer durables are goods purchased by households that last a relatively long time and are purchased infrequently. E.g., refrigerators & leisure goods and services.

Rate of interest is a charge for borrowing money and a payment for lending money.
