4.7.5 Policies to control inflation

  • Contractionary monetary policy: Raising interest rates or restricting money supply to reduce borrowing and consumer spending, directly curbing demand-pull inflation.
  • Contractionary fiscal policy: Increasing direct/indirect taxes or cutting government spending to reduce total demand in the economy.
  • Supply-side policies: Improving labour productivity, education, and infrastructure to increase productive capacity, reducing cost-push inflation in the long term.
