4.6.5 Policies to reduce unemployment

  • Demand-side policies: Expansionary fiscal policy (tax cuts, public spending) and expansionary monetary policy (lower interest rates) stimulate aggregate demand to reduce cyclical unemployment, though they can risk higher inflation or budget deficits.
  • Supply-side policies: Education, job retraining programs, occupational mobility grants, and labour market deregulation reduce structural and frictional unemployment effectively in the long term, though they can be costly and time-consuming.
